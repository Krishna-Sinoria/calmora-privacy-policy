Calmora Account Deletion

Calmora respects your privacy and provides a way to request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to the Calmora support address below.
  2. Use the subject line "Calmora Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include the email address registered with your Calmora account.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Request Account Deletion

What data will be deleted?

After verifying your request, we will delete your account and associated personal data that we are not required or otherwise permitted to retain. This may include your profile information and account-linked saved content.

Data retention

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and legal purposes. If any data is retained, it will be limited to the applicable purpose and retention period.

We will review and process verified deletion requests in accordance with applicable requirements and communicate with you if additional information is needed.
